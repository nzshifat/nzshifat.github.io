Algorithmic Resource Allocation in Public Infrastructure Delivery

Nazmuzzaman Shifat, Dr. Eleanor Vance, Dr. Kazi Rahman

Public procurement contracts account for substantial GDP shares in emerging economies, yet remain susceptible to allocation inefficiency and collusion. This project analyzes the causal impact of transitioning from discretionary administrative committees to automated algorithmic scoring systems. Using administrative registry records spanning 45,000 tenders, we exploit a staggered policy rollout to estimate impacts on project completion delays, cost overruns, and small-firm vendor entry.

Spatial Spillover Effects of Climate Disasters on Rural Banking Portfolios

Nazmuzzaman Shifat (Solo-authored)

This project investigates how localized climatic extremes propagate through interconnected rural credit networks. Combining high-resolution geospatial precipitation and flood sensor data (0.05-degree resolution) with geocoded loan performance records across 1,200 rural bank branches, I demonstrate that credit contraction spreads up to 75 kilometers outside the physical disaster perimeter due to lender balance-sheet liquidity hoarding.

Generative AI Diffusion and the Dynamic Elasticity of White-Collar Wages

Nazmuzzaman Shifat & Prof. Marcus Chen

We evaluate the wage and employment trajectory of knowledge workers across four software and consulting hubs following the adoption of autonomous coding and drafting tools. Utilizing high-frequency scraped job vacancy telemetry and individual wage tracking datasets, we identify an asymmetrical compression of entry-level salary premiums contrasted with an expansion of senior architectural compensation.

Institutional Memory and Bureaucratic Turnover in Administrative Agencies

Nazmuzzaman Shifat & Research Team

How does frequent civil service rotation affect regulatory enforcement consistency and public trust? This project construct a novel 20-year longitudinal database of 18,000 civil servants, measuring organizational knowledge decay through text analysis of regulatory audit reports.
