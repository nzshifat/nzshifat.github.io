Book Chapter 2024 • Independently Published

Beyond SDG4: Reimagining the Sustainable Development Goals for a Better Future

Shifat, M. N., & Claude, M.

In Quality Education for All for a Better World: Learning from the Sustainable Development Goals (Vol. 2). Evaluates systemic gaps in universal primary and secondary education targets, examining foundational learning loss, digital inequities, and actionable governance frameworks for post-2030 development agendas.

Book Chapter 2024 • Independently Published

The Declining Trend in Finland’s PISA Reading Scores: The Way Forward

Shifat, M. N., Claude, M., & Clarebout, H.

In F. M. Reimers, J. Chia, P. Choudhury, S. Heng, N. Nguyen, & W. Warren (Eds.), Education Policy Analysis to Make the World Better (p. 330, Vol. 2). Investigates macroeconomic and pedagogical variables contributing to Finland's longitudinal reading score trajectories in OECD PISA assessments, proposing evidence-based instructional interventions.

Conference Proceeding 2024 • Center for International Higher Education, Boston College

Higher Education Admission Policies in Bangladesh: Enabling Upward Mobility or Reinforcing Inequalities?

Shifat, M. N. (Proceeding of the 2024 WES-CIHE Summer Institute)

In S. Smith (Ed.), Equitable Higher Education in Times of Disruption (proceedings of the 2024 wes-cihe summer institute) (pp. 27–29). Examines how centralized entrance examinations and coaching center dependency in Bangladesh restrict socioeconomic mobility and reinforce structural urban-rural disparities in public university enrollment.
