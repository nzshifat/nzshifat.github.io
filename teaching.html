Teaching & Pedagogy

Undergraduate and graduate courses taught across econometrics, data analytics, and public policy. Emphasis on reproducible empirical computing, real-world case studies, and hands-on coding labs.

Pedagogical Approach

My teaching philosophy centers on translating abstract mathematical frameworks into tangible, empirical inquiry. In all courses, students work with messy real-world datasets from day one—learning not only how to run regression estimators in R and Stata, but how to formulate identification strategies, detect selection biases, and communicate statistical findings with clarity to non-technical stakeholders.
